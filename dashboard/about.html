Project objectives

DA-015 · Online Learning Engagement Analysis

  1. Prepare a clean, reliable learner-activity dataset.
  2. Measure completion, quiz performance and learning time.
  3. Compare performance across modules, devices and age groups.
  4. Define a transparent engagement score and category.
  5. Identify relationships between engagement and outcomes.
  6. Provide evidence-based recommendations.

    Limitations

    • The data is synthetic: patterns reflect the simulation assumptions and must be validated on real platform data.
    • Correlation does not imply causation. Motivated learners may both study longer and score higher.
    • Missing values were imputed with module medians, which slightly reduces variance.
    • Lesson progress feeds both the engagement score and completion, so part of that link is structural; the quiz-score gap is the cleaner test.
    • Engagement weights are a reasoned, documented choice, not statistically optimised.

    Privacy

    Learner IDs are artificial (L0001–L1000). No names, emails or other personal information exist in the dataset.
